# Daily Macro & Calorie Targets
- Calories:
  - Base Daily Budget: 1,900 kcal
  - Daily Ceiling: 2,100 kcal
  - Midnight Buffer: Meals are scheduled to total 1,900 kcal, leaving 200 kcal in reserve specifically for a late-night / midnight snack.
- Protein: 180g (Equal Priority)
- Fats: 55g (Equal Priority)
- Carbohydrates: ~170g–220g (Equal Priority)
- Fiber: 30g–35g Total
  - Whole Food Fiber: 20g–25g minimum (from oats, beans, chickpea pasta, berries, veggies, high-fiber bread/cereals)
  - Gummy Supplement Cap: Maximum 10g per day

# Meal Slots
- Pre-workout
- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner
- Snacks (including midnight snack)
